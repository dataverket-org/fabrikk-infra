# Plan: Zulip at `forum.dataverket.org`

Written 2026-10-15, reworked the same day from a HelmRelease to gitless delivery, and uploads to S3. Nothing is applied.
Zulip as a platform service delivered the way zot is (decisions 011, 012 and 013, README "Gitless delivery"):
`artifacts/zulip/` is the authored directory, plain manifests with every image pinned by digest and the Secrets
sops-encrypted to the cluster key, pushed by `push.sh` into zot as `platform/zulip-config`; `apps/zulip/source.yaml` is
the `OCIRepository` on the Service name and the Kustomization that applies it, decrypting with the cluster key. Inside
the artifact the shape is Forgejo's: one CNPG instance on one Cinder volume archived to the hov1 site, its Secrets taken
by reference, Zitadel as the login, a route on the `https` listener. `docs/storage.md` and decision 018 already count
Zulip's database as one more single instance on an SSD volume; this is the plan that brings it.

## What Zulip 12 needs, checked 2026-10-15

Read from `github.com/zulip/docker-zulip` and `github.com/zulip/zulip` at main, from the chart's templates, and
checked against this cluster.

| Fact | Consequence for this plan |
|---|---|
| The image is `ghcr.io/zulip/zulip-server:12.3-0`, a rewrite: settings are `SETTING_*`, `CONFIG_*` and `SECRETS_*` env vars, and since 12.1 old names such as `DB_HOST` stop the container | Pinned by digest in `statefulset.yaml`. Anything copied from an older guide is suspect |
| The chart, `oci://ghcr.io/zulip/helm-charts/zulip` 2.3.0, renders one StatefulSet, a Service, a PVC, a ServiceAccount and a ConfigMap for post-setup scripts. The pod: ports 80, 443 and 25, one volume at `/data`, liveness and startup probes that `curl http://127.0.0.1/health` (loopback, which `/health` always trusts), and 17 env vars that wire the four backing services | Read for what the pod needs and not used, as versitygw's chart was: a handful of objects need no Helm, and plain manifests are what 012 and 028 want to see in a diff |
| The app is one pod: one container runs nginx, Django, Tornado, the queue workers and cron. Zulip scales one realm by Tornado sharding, not by replicas, and recommends a hot spare over load balancing | One replica, a StatefulSet with a `volumeClaimTemplate`, so the `ReadWriteOnce` volume cannot wedge a rollout |
| PostgreSQL 14 to 18 are supported. The database is `zulip`, owned by role `zulip`, `C.UTF-8` collation, and Zulip creates its own schema. No extension and no superuser are needed | CNPG `initdb` with `database: zulip`, `owner: zulip` and `C.UTF-8`, on Postgres 18 like the other two clusters |
| Full-text search needs three files in Postgres' `tsearch_data`: `en_us.dict`, `en_us.affix` and `zulip_english.stop`. Without them the first migration falls back to plain English stemming, with a warning, and nothing later upgrades it. Checked in `forgejo-postgres-1`: `ghcr.io/cloudnative-pg/postgresql:18.4-system-trixie` has none of the three. CNPG's image-volume extensions cannot carry them, since Postgres reads dictionaries only from its fixed `SHAREDIR` | A Postgres image of our own, the CNPG image plus a layer with the three files, built in the factory and pushed with the artifact, as `www-site` is. It must exist before the first start, because the search configuration is made once |
| RabbitMQ, memcached and Redis are what the chart's Bitnami subcharts would bring, on `bitnamilegacy/*` images its own values call no longer updated. Zulip takes each by host, port, user and password | Three small Deployments in the artifact, pinned by digest, no volumes: RabbitMQ and memcached on their official images, Valkey for Redis (decision A). Queue contents and cache are transient, and Zulip's backup docs leave them out |
| Zulip long-polls, so the proxy must allow reads well over 60 s and pass `Host`, `X-Forwarded-For` and `X-Forwarded-Proto`; the proxy's pods must be in `LOADBALANCER_IPS`. Envoy's pods here are in `10.244.0.0/16` | `httproute.yaml` with `timeouts.request: 0s`, as zot and versitygw set; `LOADBALANCER_IPS: 10.244.0.0/16` |
| Uploads go to `/data`, or to S3 with `S3_AUTH_UPLOADS_BUCKET`, `S3_AVATAR_BUCKET`, `S3_ENDPOINT_URL` and `S3_ADDRESSING_STYLE: path`. boto3 1.43.74 and tusd 2.9.1 do the S3 work: put, get, head, delete, batch delete, list, presigned get, multipart, and copy onto itself to rewrite headers. Zulip's own nginx fetches uploads from the presigned URL's host over `https` on 443; browsers fetch avatars and emoji unsigned from the endpoint, and export links presigned | Decision B below: S3 on the osl1 gateway, by its public name |
| Generic OIDC is `GenericOpenIdConnectBackend` with `SOCIAL_AUTH_OIDC_ENABLED_IDPS`, a dict that names `get_secret("social_auth_oidc_secret")`, and the callback `https://<host>/complete/oidc/`. The entrypoint writes a `SETTING_` value that starts with `{` into `settings.py` as Python | One `SETTING_` env var plus `SECRETS_social_auth_oidc_secret` from a Secret. Inferred from `entrypoint.sh`, so proven in step 6, with `ZULIP_CUSTOM_SETTINGS` as the fallback |
| On first start the image generates every secret not given (`secret_key`, `avatar_salt`, `zulip_org_key`, `camo_key`, …) into `/data/zulip-secrets.conf`. Losing `secret_key` logs everyone out; losing `avatar_salt` breaks every avatar | The ones a restore needs are generated up front and pinned in `zulip-secrets.enc.yaml`, as `forgejo-security.enc.yaml` pins Forgejo's |
| The image's own nightly `pg_dump` lands on `/data/backups` and is kept forever; its client is built for Postgres 14 and may refuse an 18 server | `AUTO_BACKUP_ENABLED: "False"`. The backup is CNPG's |

Not confirmed upstream, and each proven in its own step: that `CONFIG_postgresql__version` sets the version
`zulip.conf` records; that S3 uploads work against versitygw (decision B's proofs); that Valkey serves Zulip as
Redis does (decision A); how `LOCAL_UPLOADS_DIR` is unset through the environment.

## Decisions

**A. Redis or Valkey.** Decided 2026-10-15: Valkey. Nobody has validated the pair, so we are the validators.

- Upstream ships and tests only Redis. "valkey" appears nowhere in `zulip/zulip` at tag 12.3, in `docker-zulip`, or
  in any issue or PR of the `zulip` org. Zulip's own installer puts `redis-server` on every supported OS, Debian 13
  included, where Valkey is also packaged.
- What Zulip asks of the server, read at tag 12.3, is all inside Redis 7.2, the line Valkey forked from with a
  promise of compatibility (`valkey.io/topics/migration`): `redis.StrictRedis(host, port, password, db=0)`, so
  password-only AUTH, no ACL user, no TLS, no modules; and one Lua script, the rate limiter, which calls only
  `TIME`, `EXISTS`, `PTTL`, `HGET`, `HSET` and `EXPIRE`. Since 12.0 Tornado's rate limiting runs on it too, so the
  script is on the hot path.
- `scripts/setup/generate_secrets.py` runs `CONFIG SET requirepass` only when it has to invent a Redis password.
  This plan gives one as `SECRETS_redis_password`, so the call never happens and the server needs no `CONFIG`.
- Public setups running Zulip on Valkey: four, all homelab or templates, none with results written down. Two run
  12.x on `valkey/valkey:9.1`: `rajubhimani/homeserver` (12.3-0 on 9.1.2, compose and Argo CD manifests, moved to
  Valkey 2026-10-03) and `k5-mot/inferlab` (12.2-0 on 9.1.1, compose). `chrishenn/homelab` runs an older image on
  `valkey:alpine`, and `stackblaze/stack-templates` has a Kubero template on 11.6 with a Valkey 8.0 cluster. No
  third-party chart, YunoHost, Cloudron or NixOS packaging uses Valkey, and nobody runs the official chart's
  `externalRedis` against it.

The choice: Valkey, `valkey/valkey:9.1` pinned by digest, `--requirepass` from `zulip-secrets.enc.yaml`, no
persistence, Service `zulip-redis` on 6379 so every name Zulip's docs use still reads true. Its data is rate-limit
state and short-lived tokens, so a swap either way is a one-image change and loses nothing. Proven in step 5, with
Redis as the fallback the same day if any proof fails: the rate limiter answers (repeated failed logins are throttled,
and `SCRIPT EXISTS` finds its sha), a long-poll is served, and the server log shows no unknown command.

**B. Uploads.** Decided 2026-10-15: S3 on the osl1 gateway from day one, Zulip its first writer
(`docs/plans/2026-10-s3-osl1.md`, steps 5, 6 and 9). Nothing to migrate later, and `/data` holds no user data.

- The gateway runs v1.9.0 first. v1.8.0 stores `Content-Encoding: aws-chunked` on every object written with a
  trailing checksum (versitygw #2398), which boto3 sends by default, and with a bucket policy in place it lets the
  owner do only what the policy grants (#2495). Both are fixed in v1.9.0. `S3_SKIP_CHECKSUM` stays off, and is the
  fallback if a proof finds `XAmzContentSHA256Mismatch`.
- Zulip points at the public name, never the Service: its nginx keeps only the hostname of a presigned URL and
  fetches over `https` on 443, browsers load avatars from the endpoint, and an export link is signed for the host it
  names. So the pod reaches `dataverket.s3.osl1.dvkt.no` back through Envoy, which is proven, not assumed.
- The account and buckets are made with the osl1 `bin/user`, the avatar bucket's policy put by the `zulip` account
  itself through `sops exec-env` on `s3-zulip.enc.yaml`.
- Backup is the gap it was on `/data`: Cinder's three copies until the osl1 plan's kopia step.

The proofs, `artifacts/zulip/s3proof`, a Go program on the AWS SDK versions tusd v2.9.1 builds with (`s3` v1.96.0), the
client Zulip uploads attachments through, path-style, as the `zulip` account against the gateway before step 5: a plain
put leaves no `Content-Encoding: aws-chunked` on the object; a presigned get with `ResponseContentDisposition` returns
that header, and a 5 MiB range request answers 206; an anonymous get is 200 on `zulip-avatars` and 403 on
`zulip-uploads`, while the account can still put, delete and list in both; a copy onto itself with
`MetadataDirective=REPLACE` changes `Content-Type` and `Content-Disposition`; a multipart upload made the way tusd makes
it completes; a batch delete removes several keys and a listing pages. Django's boto3 is the one client not exercised
before Zulip runs, so the proofs in Zulip after step 5 settle it: an attachment uploaded through tusd, thumbnailed and
downloaded, an avatar changed and shown, and a data export downloaded.

Run 2026-10-15 against versitygw v1.9.0: 13 of 13 passed, the 2 GiB single PUT among them in 9 min 36 s;
`ListObjectsV2` carries `KeyCount`, and `proof/` was empty afterwards in both buckets. The avatar policy is in place.

**C. The name.** Decided 2026-10-15: `forum.dataverket.org`. It is set once: Zulip's realm URLs, the OIDC
callback and every link anyone pastes carry it.

## Steps

1. **The Postgres image.** `artifacts/zulip/postgres-image.sh`: `crane append` of one layer onto
   `ghcr.io/cloudnative-pg/postgresql:18.4-system-trixie` pinned by digest, in crane's in-memory registry as
   `site.sh` builds, so the digest is known before anything is pushed. The layer holds `en_us.dict` and `en_us.affix`
   from Debian trixie's `hunspell-en-us` and `zulip_english.stop` from Zulip's source at tag 12.3, each fetched by
   URL and checked against a sha256 in git, landing in `/usr/share/postgresql/18/tsearch_data/` readable by uid 26.
   Proof: a throwaway CNPG `Cluster` on the image in a scratch namespace, where `CREATE TEXT SEARCH DICTIONARY …
   (TEMPLATE = ispell, DictFile = en_us, AffFile = en_us, StopWords = zulip_english)` succeeds. A Postgres bump is a
   new base digest and a rebuild, which the artifact's README states.
2. **The buckets.** On hov1, `cnpg-zulip` and its account with `backup/versitygw/bin/user`, by a person at the
   site; on osl1, the account `zulip` with `zulip-uploads` and `zulip-avatars` through the osl1 `bin/user`, once
   the gateway runs v1.9.0, and the avatar policy. Each key pair goes through `sops --encrypt` into
   `artifacts/zulip/s3-cnpg-zulip.enc.yaml` and `artifacts/zulip/s3-zulip.enc.yaml` and never touches a disk in
   clear. Proof: `hov1-s3 check` and `osl1-s3 check` find each bucket owned by its account, and decision B's S3
   proofs pass. The osl1 half done 2026-10-15: the account, both buckets, the policy, and the proofs passed; the
   hov1 bucket and both `check` runs remain.
3. **The Zitadel app.** `ZITADEL_PROJECTS` gets `zulip`, `task admin:zitadel-operator-key RENEW=1`, then
   `zitadel-project ensure` and `zitadel-app ensureOidc` from definitions that name the operator key, with the
   redirect `https://forum.dataverket.org/complete/oidc/`. The client secret is sealed by a person into
   `artifacts/zulip/zulip-zitadel-oidc.enc.yaml` (operator rule 3); no value reaches a data record.
4. **The artifact, the database only.** `artifacts/zulip/` with `namespace.yaml`, `hov1-s3.yaml`,
   `s3-cnpg-zulip.enc.yaml`, `postgres.yaml`, `backup.yaml`, `postgres-image.sh` and `push.sh`; `apps/zulip/source.yaml`
   and `zulip` in `apps/kustomization.yaml`. Applying is a commit, `push.sh` from that commit, and Flux. Proof: the
   cluster is healthy on the built image, the first base backup is on hov1, WAL archiving runs; then `bootstrap`
   switches to recovery, committed and pushed again.
5. **The app.** `services.yaml`, the Secrets, `statefulset.yaml` with the 17 wiring vars the chart would set plus
   `SETTING_EXTERNAL_HOST`, `SETTING_ZULIP_ADMINISTRATOR`, `CONFIG_postgresql__version: "18"`, `AUTO_BACKUP_ENABLED:
   "False"`, `LOADBALANCER_IPS`, the mailer and the `S3_*` settings, `service.yaml` and `httproute.yaml`; committed and
   pushed. Proof: the pod is `Ready`, `https://forum.dataverket.org/` answers, and in the database `english_us_hunspell`
   exists and `zulip.english_us_search` uses it, which rules out the fallback; and decision A's three proofs for Valkey,
   and decision B's proofs in Zulip.
6. **The first organization and the login.** `manage.py generate_realm_creation_link` in the pod, the realm made
   with a person's email as owner, then OIDC proven end to end with a second account, then `EmailAuthBackend` off in
   one more push. Proof: a long-poll survives two minutes through Envoy, and a message sent from one browser appears
   in another with no reload.
7. **A restore test.** The Forgejo restore of 2026-09-21 again (`backup/restore-test/`), for `zulip-postgres` into
   a scratch namespace, on the built image: table count, users and the newest message equal production at the same
   moment, and full-text search answers.
8. **Documentation.** `artifacts/zulip/README.md`, in the shape of `artifacts/www/README.md`: contents, ship a change,
   upgrade (a new image digest, and `CONFIG_postgresql__version` with the server), rebuild. `docs/storage.md` gets
   the two volumes; `backup/README.md` the source, the bucket and the restore; `README.md`'s Names table and its
   "Gitless delivery" list `zulip`. The two operating models go in. Then this plan is removed and its state lives in
   those files.

## Not in this plan

- Backup of the uploads beyond Cinder's copies: the osl1 plan's kopia step.
- Push notifications to the mobile apps through Zulip's bouncer service: needs `zulip_org_id` and `zulip_org_key`
  registered with zulip.com, a choice about what leaves the site.
- Tornado sharding, a hot spare, PGroonga: none is needed at this size.
- Signing: `push.sh` signs when `vaults/operator/osl1/cosign.enc.json` exists, as every artifact's does, and the
  `verify` block is switched on with the rest.
